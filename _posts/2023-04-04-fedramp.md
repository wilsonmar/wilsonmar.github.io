In 2011 a "cloud first" policy was defined in the Federal Risk and Authorization Program (FedRAMP) <a target="_blank" href="https://cio.gov/wp-content/uploads/downloads/2012/09/Federal-Cloud-Computing-Strategy.pdf">[pdf]</a> where federal agencies make use of cloud service providers (CSPs) given authority to operate (ATO) after receiving system authorization from a security assessment conducted by an independent 3PAO (Third-Party Assessor Organization). 

<a target="_blank" href="https://youtu.be/AWqcLWofvf4">VIDEO</a> <a target="_blank" href="https://www.fedramp.gov/assets/resources/training/200-A-FedRAMP-Training-FedRAMP-System-Security-Plan-SSP-Required-Documents.pdf">PDF</a>: A <strong>System Security Plan (SSP)</strong> -- submitted in both Word and PDF formats -- is required by the
OMB Security Authorization of Information Systems in Cloud Computing
<a target="_blank" href="https://cio.gov/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/fedrampmemo.pdf">[pdf]</a>. 

Each SSP authorization package can be in a machine-readable (JSON or XML format) <a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/usnistgov/OSCAL/">https://github.com/usnistgov/OSCAL</a> Open Security Controls Assessment Language - based on <a target="_blank" href="https://www.fedramp.gov/using-the-fedramp-oscal-resources-and-templates/">templates</a> created based on <a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/GSA/fedramp-automation/blob/master/documents/FedRAMP_OSCAL_Registry.xlsx">this Excel xlsx file</a> which defines fields (extensions), identifiers, and values in the <a target="_blank" href="https://github.com/GSA/fedramp-automation">FedRAMP Registry at https://github.com/GSA/fedramp-automation</a>. The template is from FedRAMP PMO and <a target="_blank" href="https://www.nist.gov/oscal/">NIST</a>.

FedRAMP (Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program)
is essentially FISMA (Federal Information Security Modernization Act) of <a target="_blank" href="https://www.congress.gov/bill/113th-congress/senate-bill/2521">2014</a>
for cloud services.

FISMA requires that agencies <strong>authorize</strong> the <a target="_blank" href="https://marketplace.fedramp.gov/#!/products?sort=productName">300+ information system products</a> that they use.
OMB (Office of Management and Budget) <a target="_blank" href="https://www.cio.gov/policies-and-priorities/circular-a-130/">Circular A-130</a> states that when agencies implement FISMA, they must use National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) standards and guidelines.

FedRAMP was established in 2011 to provide to all federal agencies using cloud services
a common set of security requirements to store, process, and transmit data.

Once a cloud service provider (CSP) obtains an Authority To Operate (ATO) under FedRAMP, 
for all agencies of the United States federal government accepts.

FedRAMP defines standards for security assessment, authorization, and continuous monitoring.

The FedRAMP Program Management Office (PMO) provides training, guidance, and advisory support to CSPs, helping them navigate the FedRAMP process and understand the requirements.

## FedRAMP Marketplace Designations
1. <strong>Ready</strong> after a Readiness Assessment Report (RAR) is submitted to the FedRAMP PMO.
2. <strong>In-Process</strong> after a Security Assessment Plan (SAP) is submitted to the FedRAMP PMO.
3. <strong>Authorized</strong> after a Security Assessment Report (SAR) is submitted to the FedRAMP PMO.
<br /><br />

## Processes

1. Readiness Assessment
   1. RAR Development
   2. FedRAMP PMO Review of RAR
   3. Remediation of RAR Findings (if needed)
   4. STATUS: FedRAMP Marketplace Designation - Ready
   <br /><br />
   Pre-Authorization:
   5. Partnership Establishment with 3PAO
   6. Authorization Planning
   7. Kickoff Meeting
   8. STATUS: FedRAMP Marketplace Designation - In Process
   <br /><br />
2. Authorization of Security Authorization Package:
   * SSP (System Security Plan)
   * SAR (Security Authorization Package)
   * POA&M (Plan of Action and Milestones)
   <br /><br />
   Agency Authorization Process:
   * Agency Review of SAR (Security Authorization Package)
   * SAR Debrief
   * Remediation of SAR Findings (if needed)
   * Agency Final Review
   * FedRAMP PMO Review of Agency Final Review
   * STATUS: FedRAMP Marketplace Designation - Authorized
   <br /><br />
3. Continuous Monitoring
   * CA-1 - Annual Assessment of procedures annually; of policies every 3 years
   * Remediation of Annual Assessment Findings (if needed)
