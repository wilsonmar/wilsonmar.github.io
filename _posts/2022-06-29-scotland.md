## Preparations for Travel

1. Get a US Passport months ahead because it can take months.

   No visa is needed to travel to Scotland from the US.

1. COVID restrictions?

1. Mobile service

   Most US carriers (AT&T, Verizon, etc.) chargest exhorbitant <strong>roaming fees</strong> for voice, data, SMS text.

   T-Mobile offers an international roaming plan.
   However, T-Mobile may not have wide enough coverage you need in Scottland.

   Many hotels and pubs have wifi, but it's a hassle to get passwords at each location.
   Then you can use WhatsApp to communicate via WiFi.
   But you can get a mifi device plus.
   However, MiFi devices have limited battery life.

   You can get a local SIM in Scotland for about £20 for 12 GB of data.
   There are plans for $10/day.
   But that means you'll have a different phone number.

   If you give out a Google Voice number of yours to others, you can switch to a local SIM on your phone at https://voice.google.com
