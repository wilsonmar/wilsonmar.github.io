Splunk has been around since the 1980's so is firmly entrenched in many datacenters.
Splunk is a software utility for <strong>machine log data</strong> collection, indexing, and visualization for "operational intelligence". 
Splunk can ingest almost all technologies (on-prem, clouds, databases, etc.) for use by SOC (Security Operations Centers) who correlate what's going on across the vast landscape of technologies.

Splunk stores data in <strong>indexes</strong> organized in directories and files.
Splunk compresses data in flat files using their own proprietary format which are read by 
Splunk Processing Language (SPL).

It is used to:

* Collect and Index Log Data: Index streaming log data from all your distributed systems regardless of format or location.

* Visualize Trends

* Zoom in and out on timelines to automatically reveal trends, spikes and patterns and click to drill down into search results.

* Issue alerts (based on AIOps)
