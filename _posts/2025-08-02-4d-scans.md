## Use Case

Imagine sending a tiny drone into a hotel room.
A minute later, you have a list of what was left behind and
what needs to be replenished, with an estimate of how long it would take,
with and without robots doing the work.

https://www.linkedin.com/learning/from-drone-to-autocad-the-workflow/working-with-point-clouds-in-autocad

Welcome to the world of 3D data processing: spatial algorithms on digital twin semantic representation, statistical analysis, and computational geometry that transforms raw point cloud data into actionable intelligence.

3D change detection finds meaningful differences while filtering out everything else.

https://medium.com/data-science-collective/smart-3d-change-detection-python-tutorial-for-point-clouds-0dfd9945eb6a

"traditional methods of tracking these changes remain painfully manual, error-prone, and time-consuming"

a Python-powered system that:

Automatically aligns multi-temporal 3D scans with millimeter precision
Identifies missing or damaged building components in seconds
Generates reports that stakeholders understand
Scales from single buildings to entire infrastructure networks

Algorithms and techniques enable spatial reasoning and decision making.
