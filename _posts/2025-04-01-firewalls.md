Large businesses install firewalls in their corporate networks such as from Palo Alto or Cisco.

Homes and small businesses use "consumer-grade" firewalls to:
   * Block annoying ads 
   * Block malicious websites
   * Block ports
 
## Block malicious websites

The first line of defense is to configure the DNS on each device (laptop, phone, tablet) to use a DNS server that blocks known malicious websites and doesn't track your online activity. Google's DNS is quick, and blocks, but sells your online activity.


The second line of defense is to use a consumer-grade firewall that blocks ports.


from brands such as Linksys, Netgear, or Draytek.

Firewalla is a consumer-grade firewall that is also a router.
