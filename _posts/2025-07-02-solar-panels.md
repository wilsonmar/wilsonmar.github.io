1. What difference does it make to tilt the solar panel?

   <a target="_blank" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mUpTRtO4eN0&t=5m53s">This video</a> from 
   <a target="_blank" href="https://www.youtube.com/@everydaysolar">Everyday Solar</a>
   says, over 3 hours, a horizontally placed panel yielding 313.1 Wh, when tilted to 29 degrees yielded 388.4 Wh.
   That 75.3 Wh difference is a 29% improvement, which shortens charge time.

   <a target="_blank" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r7HwQdssbas&11m12s">VIDEO</a>:
   Compared with ground mounting, the single axis kits from <a target="_blank" href="https://ca.eco-worthy.com/">eco-worthy.com in Canada</a>
   yielded about 6% more. The dual axis kit yielded 8%-14% more.

   <a target="_blank" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r7HwQdssbas&11m12s">VIDEO</a>:
   Tracking provides higher yield especially in early mornings and late afternoons when the sun is not as directly overhead
