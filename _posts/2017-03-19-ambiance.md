## Types of "Noise"

"White Noise" is a catch-all phrase for ambient sounds that help you concentrate or relax.

The types of ambient/background "white noise":

A). <a href="#Tones">Continuous tones of sound</a>.

B). <a href="#Ambient">Ambient sounds</a> such as what you hear in a cafeteria.

C). <a href="#Music">Music without lyrics (electronic or orchestral)</a>

### Pink noise

<strong>"Pink noise"</strong> has a deeper feeling and thus a more comfortable,  natural sound than white noise. In technical terms, its power spectral density is inversely proportional to the frequency of the signal, rather than equal intensity to it. This means the lower frequencies in pink noise are louder than the higher frequencies.

An even deeper sound than Pink noise is
<strong>Brown or Brownian noise</strong> is based on <a target="_blank" href="https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Brownian_motion">Brownian Motion</a> -- the random motion of particles suspended in a fluid (a liquid or a gas) resulting from their collision with the fast-moving molecules in the fluid. It's also called "Red noise"?
