This provides an example of how vendors of software can provide a customer-centric use of CAIQ.

If you are a security product/service vendor to cloud providers (such as HashiCorp Consul, Vault, Terraform):

> PROTIP: Phrase what your customer can say in a CAIQ after installing your product. That would save them the agony of drafting paragraphs for their auditors. That's the customer-centric approach.

Cloud providers make use of the <a target="_blank" href="https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/download/artifacts/star-level-1-security-questionnaire-caiq-v4/">Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) Consensus Assessment Initiative Questionnaire (CAIQ) v4</a>. Answer spreadsheets from notable cloud providers:

   * <a target="_blank" href="https://downloads.esri.com/resources/enterprisegis/AGOL_CSA_CAIQ.pdf">PDF: ESRI's answers</a> references both SOC2, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.iso.org/isoiec-27001-information-security.html">ISO 27001:2018</a>, and FedRamp 880-53.
   * <a target="_blank" href="https://d1.awsstatic.com/whitepapers/compliance/CSA_Consensus_Assessments_Initiative_Questionnaire.pdf">PDF: Amazon's answers</a>
   * https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry/microsoft/
   * https://www.oracle.com/a/ocom/docs/oci-corporate-caiq.pdf
   * https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/sep_2021_caiq_self_assessment.pdf
   * https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry/atlassian/services/jira-and-confluence-cloud/
   <br /><br />

The spreadsheet of answers from various cloud providers incorporate <a target="_blank" href="https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix/">CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM)</a> of each control expressed as a <strong>task</strong>.

There are both full and lite editions of CAIQ. 

## Categories

The full 261 questions in v4.0 map validates controls in 17 control categories:

1. <a href="#A%A-"><tt>A%A</tt></a>  = Audit Assurance & Compliance
1. <a href="#AIS-"><tt>AIS</tt></a>  = Application & Interface Security
1. <a href="#BCR-"><tt>BCR</tt></a>  = Business Continuing Management & Operational Resilience
1. <a href="#CCC-"><tt>CCC</tt></a>  = Change Control & Configuration Management
1. <a href="#CEK-"><tt>CEK</tt></a>  = Cryptography, Encryption, and Key Management
1. <a href="#DCS-"><tt>DCS</tt></a>  = Datacenter Security
1. <a href="#DSP-"><tt>DSP</tt></a>  = Data Security & Privacy Lifecycle Management
1. <a href="#GRC-"><tt>GRC</tt></a>  = Governance, Risk Management, and Compliance
1. <a href="#HRS-"><tt>HRS</tt></a>  = Human Resources
1. <a href="#IAM-"><tt>IAM</tt></a>  = Identity & Access Management
1. <a href="#IPY-"><tt>IPY</tt></a>  = Interoperability & Portability
1. <a href="#IVS-"><tt>IVS</tt></a>  = Infrastructure & Virtualization Security
1. <a href="#LOG-"><tt>LOG</tt></a>  = Logging and Monitoring
1. <a href="#SEF-"><tt>SEF</tt></a>  = Security Incident Management, E-Discovery, and Cloud Forensics
1. <a href="#STA-"><tt>STA</tt></a>  = Supply Chain Management, Transparancy, and Accountability
1. <a href="#TVM-"><tt>TVM</tt></a>  = Threat and Vulnerability Management 
1. <a href="#UEM-"><tt>UEM</tt></a>  = Universal Endpoint Management

Each category has a question related to its <strong>documentation</strong>, in the form of:

   * Are ____ policies, procedures, and standards established, documented, approved, communicated, applied, evaluated, and maintained?
   <br /><br />

Each category has a question related to <strong>scheduling of annual review</strong>, in the form of:

   * Are ____ policies and procedures reviewed and updated at least annually?
