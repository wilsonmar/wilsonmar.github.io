Cloud vendors make use of the <a target="_blank" href="https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/download/artifacts/star-level-1-security-questionnaire-caiq-v4/">Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) Consensus Assessment Initiative Questionnaire (CAIQ) v4</a> 

Notable answer spreadsheets:

   * <a target="_blank" href="https://downloads.esri.com/resources/enterprisegis/AGOL_CSA_CAIQ.pdf">PDF: ESRI's answers</a> references both SOC2, <a target="_blank" href="https://www.iso.org/isoiec-27001-information-security.html">ISO 27001:2018</a>, and FedRamp 880-53.
   * <a target="_blank" href="https://d1.awsstatic.com/whitepapers/compliance/CSA_Consensus_Assessments_Initiative_Questionnaire.pdf">PDF: Amazon's answers</a>
   * https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry/microsoft/
   * https://www.oracle.com/a/ocom/docs/oci-corporate-caiq.pdf
   * https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/sep_2021_caiq_self_assessment.pdf
   * https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry/atlassian/services/jira-and-confluence-cloud/
   <br /><br />

The spreadsheet of answers from various cloud vendors incorporate <a target="_blank" href="https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix/">CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM)</a> of each control expressed as a <strong>task</strong>.

There are both full and lite editions of CAIQ. 
The full 261 questions in v4.0 map validates controls in 17 control categories:

1. <a href="#A%A-">A%A</a>  = Audit Assurance & Compliance
1. <a href="#AIS-">AIS</a>  = Application & Interface Security
1. <a href="#BCR-">BCR</a>  = Business Continuing Management & Operational Resilience
1. <a href="#CCC-">CCC</a>  = Change Control & Configuration Management
1. <a href="#CEK-">CEK</a>  = Cryptography, Encryption, and Key Management
1. <a href="#DCS-">DCS</a>  = Datacenter Security
1. <a href="#DSP-">DSP</a>  = Data Security & Privacy Lifecycle Management
1. <a href="#GRC-">GRC</a>  = Governance, Risk Management, and Compliance
1. <a href="#HRS-">HRS</a>  = Human Resources
1. <a href="#IAM-">IAM</a>  = Identity & Access Management
1. <a href="#IPY-">IPY</a>  = Interoperability & Portability
1. <a href="#IVS-">IVS</a>  = Infrastructure & Virtualization Security
1. <a href="#LOG-">LOG</a>  = Logging and Monitoring
1. <a href="#SEF-">SEF</a>  = Security Incident Management, E-Discovery, and Cloud Forensics
1. <a href="#STA-">STA</a>  = Supply Chain Management, Transparancy, and Accountability
1. <a href="#TVM-">TVM</a>  = Threat and Vulnerability Management 
1. <a href="#UEM-">UEM</a>  = Universal Endpoint Management

### A&A = Audit Assurance & Compliance

1. <a name="A&A-01.1">A&A-01.1</a> - Audit and Assurance Policy and Procedures<br /><br >
	Are audit and assurance policies, procedures, and standards established, documented, approved, communicated, applied, evaluated, and maintained?

1. <a name="A&A-01.2">A&A-01.2</a> - <br /><br >
	Are audit and assurance policies, procedures, and standards reviewed and updated at least annually?

1. <a name="A&A-02.1">A&A-02.1</a> - Independent Assessments<br /><br >
   Are audit and assurance policies, procedures, and standards reviewed and updated at least annually?

1. <a name="A&A-02.1">A&A-02.2</a> - <br /><br >
   Are independent audit and assurance assessments conducted according to relevant standards at least annually?

1. <a name="A&A-03.1">A&A-03.1</a> - Risk Based Planning Assessment<br /><br >
   Are independent audit and assurance assessments performed according to risk-based plans and policies?

1. <a name="A&A-04.1">A&A-04.1</a> - Requirements Compliance<br /><br >
   Is compliance verified regarding all relevant standards, regulations, legal/contractual, and statutory requirements applicable to the audit?

1. <a name="A&A-05.1">A&A-05.1</a> - Audit Management Process<br /><br >
   Is an audit management process defined and implemented to support audit planning, risk analysis, security control assessments, conclusions, remediation schedules, report generation, and reviews of past reports and supporting evidence?

1. <a name="A&A-06.1">A&A-06.1</a> - Remediation<br /><br >

   Is the remediation status of audit findings reviewed and reported to relevant stakeholders?

   ### GRC = Governance, Risk Management, and Compliance

   ANSWER: We use Consul to control routing of traffic among apps based on Intentions and ACL (Access Control List) in the Consul KV (Key Value) store. Intentions determine the identities which an app may communicate with. ACLs determine whether specific permissions (such a Read, Write) are allowed or denied.

   ### UEM = Universal Endpoint Management

   DEFINITION: Unified Endpoint Management (UEM) allows IT to manage, secure, and deploy corporate resources and applications on any device from a single console.
   UEM "unifies" legacy mobile device management (MDM) by incorporating IoT and other new device technologies.

1. <a name="UEM-02.1">UEM-02.1</a>	- Application and Service Approval<br /><br />
   Is there a defined, documented, applicable and evaluated list containing approved services, applications, and the sources of applications (stores) acceptable for use by endpoints when accessing or storing organization-managed data?

1. <a name="UEM-03.1">UEM-03.1</a>	- Compatibility<br /><br />
   Is a process defined and implemented to validate endpoint device compatibility with operating systems and applications?

1. <a name="UEM-04.1">UEM-04.1</a>	- Endpoint Inventory<br /><br />
   Is an inventory of all endpoints used and maintained to store and access company data?

1. <a name="UEM-05.1">UEM-05.1</a>	- Endpoint Management<br /><br />
   Are processes, procedures, and technical measures defined, implemented and evaluated, to enforce policies and controls for all endpoints permitted to access systems and/or store, transmit, or process organizational data?

1. <a name="UEM-06.1">UEM-06.1</a>	- Automatic Lock Screen<br /><br />
   Are all relevant interactive-use endpoints configured to require an automatic lock screen?

1. <a name="UEM-07.1">UEM-07.1</a>	- Operating Systems<br /><br />

1. <a name="UEM-08.1">UEM-08.1</a>	- Storage Encryption<br /><br />
   Is information protected from unauthorized disclosure on managed endpoints with storage encryption?

1. <a name="UEM-09.1">UEM-09.1</a>	- Anti-Malware Detection and Prevention<br /><br />
   Are anti-malware detection and prevention technology services configured on managed endpoints?

1. <a name="UEM-10.1">UEM-10.1</a>	- Software Firewall<br /><br />

1. <a name="UEM-11.1">UEM-11.1</a>	- Data Loss Prevention<br /><br />
   Are managed endpoints configured with data loss prevention (DLP) technologies and rules per a risk assessment?

1. <a name="UEM-12.1">UEM-12.1</a>	- Remote Locate<br /><br />
   Are remote geolocation capabilities enabled for all managed mobile endpoints?

1. <a name="UEM-13.1">UEM-13.1</a>	- Remote Wipe<br /><br />
