## Defensive Tactics

1. Employee training against phishing attacks.

1. Use MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) for all logins.

1. Setup secure video surveillance of remote devices to provide history of access.

1. Configure every node with certificates for two-way encryption using<strong>mTLS</strong> (Mutual TLS) between every node for all network connections.

1. <strong>Segment</strong> the network into zones, with a firewall between each zone, and only allow the minimum ports and protocols needed for each zone. This limits lateral movement by attackers.

1. Send alerts to SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) for irregular logins, when a device is added to or removed from the network, when data streams are larger or smaller than normal, etc.
